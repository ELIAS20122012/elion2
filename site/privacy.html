Privacy Policy — Elias OS

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Elias OS is a personal application used only by its owner, Elias Farahani (Elion Group). This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

  • Gmail (read-only): sender, subject, date and a short snippet of recent inbox messages.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): titles, times and locations of upcoming events.

Elias OS never sends, deletes or modifies email or calendar data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to create a private daily summary for the owner, delivered to the owner's own Telegram chat. Verification codes and one-time links are removed before processing. To write the summary, the data may be processed by the AI services the owner subscribes to (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini) under their respective terms; it is not used to train models by Elias OS.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored locally. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the summary described above.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
