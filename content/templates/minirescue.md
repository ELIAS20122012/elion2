# Mini Rescue — giant hand saves a tiny village (daily series, 14 s)

CORE IDEA: tiny miniature people face a dramatic problem → a giant REAL human hand fixes it with ONE simple
real-world object or action → the tiny people celebrate. Clever, visually satisfying, instantly understandable.

AUTO MODE: you are not chatting. Invent 10 fresh scenarios in your head (each ≤ 8 words, e.g.
"🔥 Campfire spreads toward tents → glass of water"), avoid the recent episodes listed below, pick the most
visual and clever one, and produce the prompts for it.

## Style (every frame) — EXACTLY like the reference image (content/templates/minirescue.ref.jpg)
- Photorealistic miniature diorama / tilt-shift photography — NOT cartoon, NOT CGI-cute, NOT clay.
- High-angle wide view looking down on a detailed tiny European village: stone lanes, cobblestones, small
  stone/timber houses with slate roofs, vegetable gardens, fences, a river and bridges, hills in the background.
- Golden-hour / sunset light with dramatic warm clouds, strong tilt-shift blur at top and bottom, rich real textures.
- Tiny realistic human villagers (normal proportions, rustic work clothes), 3–8 people, small in the frame,
  busy and believable (pushing, carrying, holding on, rescuing each other).
- A clear, dramatic village-scale problem (river flooding the lane, cart stuck in mud, bridge collapsed,
  wall falling, road blocked by a boulder, crops washed away…).
- No text, logo, watermark, captions or UI. Vertical 9:16.
- The giant hand is a bare, realistic adult human hand — the only giant element.

## Image and clips (camera completely STATIC — no camera movement, no cuts)
image1 = the problem moment. NO hand visible. The villagers react visibly to the danger (seen from above, their poses show panic and effort),
         the danger is behind/beside them and they react to it (flee toward the camera, push, hold on…).
         Format: "Photorealistic miniature diorama, tilt-shift photography, high-angle wide view of a tiny European
         village at golden hour. [3–8] tiny realistic villagers are [struggling action] near [object/location].
         [Problem: scale, motion, danger]. [Environment details]. Strong tilt-shift blur, warm sunset light,
         no text, no watermarks."
video  = clip 1, 8 s, starts from image1. 3 sentences: the tiny characters actively struggle; a real human hand slowly
         enters from the top holding the object; the hand BEGINS the fix. The problem must NOT be solved yet when
         the clip ends (it ends mid-action).
video2 = clip 2, 6 s, continues from the exact last frame of clip 1. 3 sentences: the hand completes the fix and
         the transformation finishes; the hand exits upward; the villagers celebrate, waving up at the hand.

Physics & logic rules: one simple, gentle, physically plausible fix (place, lift, pour, cover, block, prop) —
no explosions, smoke, dust clouds, popping or destruction. Objects keep the same size and position across both
clips; nothing appears, disappears or teleports. Same number of characters throughout. Never repeat the fix twice.
The hand is ALWAYS a bare, realistic adult human hand (natural skin) — never a glove, mitten, sleeve or robot hand.
Avoid ice, smoke, fire, glass and falling snow (Veo morphs them). Water is fine as a river/flood, but the FIX uses a
solid simple object (stone, plank, log, board, cart, box, branch, rope, basket, stick, bridge piece).
The problem is already visible and STATIC at the start (stuck, blocked, broken, trapped) — not something falling or flying.
One object, one simple action; a viewer must understand the problem within 2 seconds.
Never use the words toy, collectible, figurine, LEGO or any brand/film/character name (Veo's filter rejects them).
Do not re-describe the scene in the video prompts — only motion. End EACH video prompt with:
"Static camera. Photorealistic miniature diorama with tilt-shift look throughout. The tiny villagers stay the same throughout.
Natural ambient sound — [2–3 fitting sounds]. No music."
